# Research: E-invoicing in France and the EU for a small SaaS

## Question
Reader: a founder of a small SaaS that bills businesses in Europe. Some are established in an EU country (a Belgian, French or German company); many are outside the EU (US, UK, India) and sell B2B subscriptions to EU customers. As of November 2026 they want to know: which e-invoicing mandates are live, which ones apply to them (as the seller, and through their customers), what an e-invoice has to contain, and what to change in their billing.

- https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/media/1_metier/2_professionnel/EV/2_gestion/290_facturation_electronique/guide_pratique_facturation_electronique.pdf (DGFiP, start-up guide for 1 Sep 2026; read 2026-11-21): legal calendar kept; a real invoice received by email, PDF or paper must not be rejected for that reason alone; no penalties during the start-up phase for firms on a "trajectoire sérieuse de mise en conformité", which is "ni un report ni une suspension"; fines per invoice under CGI art. 1737.

## Disagreements
- **Did France start?** Vendor and blog pages through October still ran "postponed?" headlines. The DGFiP page and start-up guide say it started on 1 Sep 2026; the soft part is enforcement (no penalties for firms making a real effort during the start-up phase), not the date.
- **Belgium and non-established companies.** Some vendor guides say "all VAT-registered businesses". The FPS Finance page is explicit that non-established taxpayers without a fixed establishment are out, even with a Belgian VAT number. We follow FPS Finance.
- **Belgian enforcement end date.** One vendor says fines from 1 Apr 2026; another mentions a self-billing tolerance to 30 Jun 2026. FPS Finance's own note covers Q1 2026 only; the post says the general tolerance covered January to March 2026 and doesn't repeat the self-billing detail.
- **Germany "mandatory since 2025".** True for receiving only; issuing stays optional until end of 2026 (end of 2027 under €800,000), and the BMF says there is no fine for a non-e-invoice, only the customer's civil claim.
- **Poland and foreign firms.** Firms disagree on when a fixed establishment exists; MF published explanations on 28 Jan 2026. A non-EU SaaS with no Polish establishment is out either way.
- **Which VAT category does a non-EU seller use for B2B services to an EU buyer?** Sources don't settle it for a non-established seller (AE reverse charge needs a seller VAT or tax ID under BR-AE-02; O "not subject to VAT" forbids one). The post doesn't pick one; it says to ask an adviser and gives the rules each needs. (Kept out of the post's claims.)
